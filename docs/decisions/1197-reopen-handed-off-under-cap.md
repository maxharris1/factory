- **Decision:** On a `ship: handoff` ticket in `handed-off` whose next review round is still under the effective cap, an approver's `kind=go` with `state=building` reopens the ticket. One transaction clears the recorded approval, counts the shipped APPROVE's round as completed, sets `fixing` and `reopen_hold`, leaves `cap_extra` alone and queues one `fix` with `kind=reopen`, keyed on the go. The fix's brief merges `origin/<base>` first, as a merge commit, and skips the merge when the base is already an ancestor of the head. After the transaction the factory moves the Linear issue out of its handoff state through part 1's reopen (#1196). A `state=planning` go, a go with no `state=`, and a go on any other `ship` mode stay `go-after-handoff`.

This replaces "a go inside the cap still changes nothing" in [118-extra-round-after-handoff.md](118-extra-round-after-handoff.md) for that one case. At or over the cap a go still follows #118: it raises `cap_extra` by 1 and sets no `reopen_hold`. An untrusted go stays `rejected-go-author`, and with `github_app.enabled` a go from a login outside `approver_logins` stays `go-not-from-approver`. A `merge-queue` ticket keeps #130's go path. A late factory `kind=verdict` after handoff stays `after-handoff`: this decision does not turn a late REVISE into a reopen. A replayed go finds its `review_events` row, queues nothing and leaves the round alone. The factory reopens Linear before it writes the `fixing` label, so a replay after a failed label write retries only the label. The completed round is never below the stored `review_round`. An old shipped APPROVE does not rewind rounds a reopen ran after it, so a ticket capped after a reopen and handed off again follows #118 on a go and answers `ready-after-cap` on a `ready`.

The reopen is one `fix` job, not an `update-from-main` followed by a fix. `_settle_update` reads an update's ready as merge-queue work, and both jobs would share one branch. The merge-only steps sit in the fix brief so one ready closes the round. The TLA+ model has `ReopenAfterHandoff` and a `reopenHold` flag. `LinearHandoff` needs `~flags.reopenHold`, every settle clears the flag, and `ReopenHoldOnlyAtWork` checks that the flag is set only while the ticket is at work.
