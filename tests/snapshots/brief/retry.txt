## Review rules (docs/SPEC.md section 8)
- One reviewer, from a different model family than the builder, gives the final factory verdict.
- That reviewer runs the project's verify skill when factory.md names one, plus interrogate and blast-radius, and may run code in a throwaway copy. That reviewer marks a finding `[blocking]` only when they have run it and seen it fail.
- There is no second factory reviewer and no factory Judge.
- Risk low: one review round; leftover minors are batched into a follow-up ticket. Risk high: up to three rounds; every extra round needs Max's own words.
- Gate retries (CI red, then fix) are not review rounds. Three CI-red cycles in a row mark the ticket stuck.
- Ship is pinned to the covered head. A reviewer never merges.
- Another agent's approval is never treated as Max's.
- `ac=met`: every acceptance criterion in the ticket is met at the reviewed head.
- `ac=unmet`: at least one acceptance criterion is not met; the verdict names which.
- `verdict=APPROVE`: ship this head as it is. Only with `ac=met` and no `[blocking]` finding.
- `verdict=REVISE`: the builder must change the PR: at least one `[blocking]` finding, or `ac=unmet`.
- `verdict=BLOCKED`: you could not review at all; say what you need.
