# Gemini Bot

## orch jobs

Gemini Bot's waiter runs `orch wait gemini-bot --autostart` as Gemini Bot's own user and login, under its restart supervisor (`deploy/waiter-service.sh gemini-bot`, or the `orch-waiter@gemini-bot` user unit), so a killed waiter comes back by itself: on each claim it starts the job itself as `orch job run gemini-bot <id> --worktree -- <command>`, with the command from `~/.config/factory/waiter-gemini-bot.yml` (the `gemini` CLI invocation Gemini Bot chose; no job or comment text is ever added to it) and the job's standard brief, plus the repo's `factory.md` context, on the harness's stdin and in `$ORCH_JOB_BRIEF`, then goes back to waiting, so no model turn and no message from another bot is needed to start a job; `orch` never chooses a harness on its own.

Each job gets exactly one handoff: once Gemini Bot's autostart waiter has claimed a job, its harness run alone runs it and posts its one `orch:v1` handoff, and a Gemini Bot turn never runs or posts for it. `orch` enforces this: a plain `orch wait gemini-bot` is refused while the autostart waiter runs, `orch job run` and `orch job done` refuse a job that waiter claimed unless they come from its own run, a repeat `orch job done` with the same handoff is a no-op and a conflicting one is refused, and when the harness exits without `orch job done` the job goes to T6 at once.

Without `--autostart`, Gemini Bot runs `orch wait gemini-bot --db "$ORCH_DB"` as a blocking shell call and spends no model tokens until that shell exits. The shell prints one JSON job line (`id`, `action`, `repo`, `ticket_id`, `source_comment_id`, `head`, `detail`, `attempt`, `timeout_seconds`, the `device` the job holds a slot on, with `remote`, and `proof`, this claim's secret token). While the job runs, `orch job run`, `orch job heartbeat` and `orch job done` for it are refused (exit 2, nothing written) unless they run with `ORCH_JOB_PROOF=<proof>` in their environment, so Gemini Bot runs each of them that way and never prints, posts, writes down or commits the proof.

Gemini Bot starts the `gemini` CLI for that job as `orch job run gemini-bot <id> --worktree -- gemini -p ...`. When running from an isolated account folder (`<home>/.factory-accounts/<id>`), `orch job run` sets `GEMINI_CLI_HOME` to that folder and scrubs every Gemini auth override (`GEMINI_API_KEY`, `GOOGLE_API_KEY`, `GOOGLE_CLOUD_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `GOOGLE_APPLICATION_CREDENTIALS`, `GOOGLE_GENAI_USE_GCA`, `GOOGLE_GENAI_USE_VERTEXAI`, `GEMINI_CLI_USE_COMPUTE_ADC`, `CLOUD_SHELL`, `GOOGLE_GEMINI_BASE_URL` and `GEMINI_CLI_CUSTOM_HEADERS`) after the login profile runs so the job authenticates from the account folder rather than an exported key. When the harness has posted its `orch:v1` handoff comment, Gemini Bot runs `orch job done gemini-bot <id> --handoff <comment-id>`; if the job ends with no handoff, it runs `orch job done gemini-bot <id> --failed` so T6 retries it.
